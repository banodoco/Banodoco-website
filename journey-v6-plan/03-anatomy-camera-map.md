# 03 — The Mushroom Anatomy & Camera Map (P1)

**Objective:** produce the handoff's "key design artifact" — one two-page spatial narrative map that becomes *"the agreed spatial truth that prevents the project from becoming six unrelated scenes."* Required **before production scene work**.

**Owner:** Motion Designer + 3D Dev, approved by Peter. **Exit gate G1** (jointly with `02`).

## Format

Not a coordinate spec. Two annotated pages (Figma, or annotated renders/screenshots of the hero + grey-box) checked into `journey-v6-plan/map/`. The resting-composition and movement matrices in the handoff (§Spatial Narrative) are the reference inputs and must remain legible against the final map.

## Page 1 — anatomy and topology (M)

- [ ] AM-1.1 The implemented Mission organism in one view (trace from the live hero, not from mockups), plus: rear cap rim, under-cap release regions, radial + secondary gills, cross-veins, stipe exterior, soil-line, irregular mycelial colony, active fairy-ring growth front, final cutaway.
- [ ] AM-1.2 Physical placement of every named element: **Arca Gidan Prize, ArtCompute, 2RP** (three spore exit regions around the rear under-cap rim — distinct sectors, all fed from the gills), **Community** (a real connected gill region), **ADOS** (convergence knot), **Hivemind** (braided persistent route), **ownership pods** (100% shared primary; two secondary), **contributor portraits** (depth-scattered field).
- [ ] AM-1.3 Connector arrows showing what grows from / feeds what: under-cap spore origins → rim paths → plumes; commons → stipe junction; colony growth front → fairy ring.
- [ ] AM-1.4 Mark the three thresholds: rear-cap discovery, move beneath the cap, soil-line crossing.
- [ ] AM-1.5 A clearly-marked **reserved area noting Equip as deferred** — not on the current path.

## Page 2 — camera journey (M)

- [ ] AM-2.1 Five resting poses: Mission (the existing hero pose, traced exactly), Inspire (rear three-quarter after a 120–180° orbit + slight push-in, copy bottom), Connect (wide low-angle in the gill chamber, copy left), Owned (underground glide, copy top-centre), Final (oblique cutaway recession, copy upper-left over the diagonal ground plane).
- [ ] AM-2.2 The continuous path between poses with dominant movement annotations (orbit / slip-under / lateral+descent / rise+recede). No roll anywhere; no full decorative revolution on the Mission→Inspire orbit.
- [ ] AM-2.3 Landscape **and portrait** framing intention for every pose (portrait poses are deliberate, per `12-platforms.md`).
- [ ] AM-2.4 Entry movement, exit movement, text position, and the occlusion/streaming opportunity at each threshold.

## Review loop

- [ ] AM-3.1 30-minute walkthrough with Peter; capture corrections; one revision round max before G1 (further refinement happens against the grey-box, where the handoff says exact splines/allocations get resolved).
- [ ] AM-3.2 Every chapter doc (`07`–`10`) links to the map; any later spatial change is made *on the map first*, then in code.

## Acceptance
- Both pages exist, are legible to someone who has never read the handoff, and carry Peter's sign-off note + date.
